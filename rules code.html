🏰 HOW TO PLAY 🏰

Protect the King and defend the castle from enemy attacks!

Objective

Be the first player to reach 5 points and save the kingdom.

How To Play

  1. Choose one of the five defenders.
  2. The computer will choose a defender.
  3. The two defenders battle.
  4. The winner earns 1 point.
  5. First to 5 points wins the game.

Battle Results

  • 🛡️ Knight defeats 🏹 Archer and 🧙 Wizard
  • 🏹 Archer defeats 🐉 Dragon and 👑 King
  • 🧙 Wizard defeats 🛡️ Knight and 🐉 Dragon
  • 🐉 Dragon defeats 🛡️ Knight and 🏹 Archer
  • 👑 King defeats 🧙 Wizard and 🐉 Dragon

Draw: If both players choose the same defender, the round is a draw and nobody gets a point.
